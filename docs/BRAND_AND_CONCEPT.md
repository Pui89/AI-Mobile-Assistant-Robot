# HOMEGUARD AI — Platform Brand and NOMAD Product Concept

## Product naming architecture

- **PUI89** — company / engineering brand.
- **HOMEGUARD AI** — evidence-driven building-condition intelligence software platform.
- **HOMEGUARD AI NOMAD** — proposed portable mobile-robot product extending the platform into home, travel and camping environments.
- **Repository** — `Pui89/Homeguard-Ai-Nomad`.

Use **HOMEGUARD AI NOMAD** consistently for the physical robot product. Do not use “NOMAD” as a replacement for the underlying HOMEGUARD AI software platform. The product name is a working brand choice; trademark and domain clearance have not been completed.

## Identity direction

The HOMEGUARD AI platform is represented by a geometric three-storey building matrix. Three clear floor plates communicate real building structure; squared vertical members communicate engineering; a bright central cyan spine and connected sensor nodes communicate the intelligence layer.

The mark deliberately avoids a roof, house, shield, lock or generic alarm icon. It should read as building intelligence and condition understanding, not only home security.

The NOMAD robot should carry the same visual identity in a compact, portable form: rounded protective body, low and wide stance, four rugged wheels, concealed mechanical details, a compact 360-degree sensor pod and controlled electric-cyan status lighting. Keep the appearance friendly, robust and professional rather than militaristic.

## NOMAD physical design targets — PROPOSED

| Parameter | Early target |
|---|---:|
| Overall height including sensor pod | Up to 50 cm |
| Main body height | 30–35 cm |
| Width | 30–35 cm |
| Length | 40–45 cm |
| Total mass | Approximately 8 kg |
| Wheel diameter | 10–13 cm |

These are design targets only. They must be checked against battery capacity, stability, wheel torque, ground clearance, sensor field of view, weather sealing, enclosure strength, thermal management and lift/carry ergonomics.

## Visual system

- Deep navy foundation: `#061426` / `#0A2038`
- Electric cyan signal: `#52E5FA` / `#83F8FF`
- Architectural blue: `#153B5C`
- White text: `#EAF7FF`
- Geometry: flat-cut edges, strong horizontal structure and clear sensor placement
- Light: glow stays inside the central intelligence spine and sensor nodes; avoid excessive external glow

## Product concept

NOMAD is intended to support three selectable operating profiles: **Home**, **Travel** and **Camping**. It is designed around owner-supervised operation, evidence reporting, local navigation, offline-first behaviour and safe-stop / conditional safe-return logic.

## Evidence boundary

All robot illustrations and physical targets show intended product direction, not a built device. Current repository software status remains FOUNDATION / SIMULATION-ONLY. No real sensor integration, trained robot model, certified alarm, structural certification, weather rating, fire resistance, explosion protection or validated field performance is claimed. Human review is required; certified protection remains primary.
